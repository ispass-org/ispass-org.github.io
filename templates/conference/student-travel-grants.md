:::note
Whether ISPASS {{YEAR}} will offer student travel grants, and in what amount,
depends on sponsorship that is still being arranged. This page describes how the
programme has worked in recent years and will be updated once the Student Travel
Grants Chair confirms the details.
:::

ISPASS has in recent years offered travel support to encourage student
participation. Applications and grant amounts are decided **case by case**;
funding is not guaranteed.

## Who can apply

All undergraduate and graduate students interested in learning about and sharing
research in systems and software performance analysis are welcome to apply.

You do **not** need to be an author or presenter of an accepted ISPASS paper to
qualify — though applications from authors and presenters receive higher
priority.

## What an application requires

- The ISPASS {{YEAR}} travel grant application form — the link will be published
  here when applications open.
- **A letter from your advisor**, uploaded with the form, which should:
  1. state that you are a full-time student pursuing a BS, MS, PhD or postdoc at
     the time of the conference, in an area covered by ISPASS;
  2. comment on the availability, or lack, of travel funds for you from other
     sources;
  3. indicate whether you are currently supported on a federal grant;
  4. commit to covering travel costs in excess of the travel grant.

  Your advisor is also welcome to comment on the broader impact of making an
  award to you.

## Conditions

:::warning
Applications submitted after the deadline, or with missing or incorrect
information, are automatically ineligible.
:::

- Awardees must **keep original receipts** for reimbursement.
- Expenses are **reimbursed after the conference**, not in advance.
- Awardees from US institutions must fly with **US-based carriers** for airfare
  to be reimbursable.
- Awardees are asked to submit a **short essay** (around two pages) describing
  what they learned at ISPASS and the highlights of the conference.

Successful applicants are notified by email.
