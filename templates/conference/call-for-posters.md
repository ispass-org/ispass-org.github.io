ISPASS invites **extended poster abstracts** covering ongoing or late-breaking
research, tools or benchmarks. The poster session is a good fit for work that is
promising but not yet complete, for negative results, and for tools you want
feedback on before a full paper.

Posters are solicited in the same [fields of interest as full
papers](/{{YEAR}}/cfp/#topics-of-interest).

Extended poster abstracts are judged primarily on their potential to enable and
amplify future research.

See the [Poster Submission Instructions](/{{YEAR}}/poster-submission/) for
formatting and length requirements.
