:::warning
**Start early.** Visa interview wait times vary enormously by country and can
run to several months. Begin the process as soon as you intend to attend, and do
not wait for a paper acceptance.
:::

Whether you need a visa to attend ISPASS {{YEAR}} depends on your nationality
and on the purpose and length of your stay.

## Entry requirements

:::note
The host country's entry requirements, any visa-waiver or electronic travel
authorization scheme, and the official government application site will be
listed here once the venue is confirmed.
:::

:::warning
Always apply through the **official government site**. Many third-party sites
charge a substantial fee for the same application.
:::

## Invitation letters

ISPASS {{YEAR}} can provide a letter of invitation to support a visa
application.

:::note
The contact address for invitation letters will be published here once the
ISPASS {{YEAR}} General Chair confirms who handles them.
:::

When requesting an invitation letter, include:

- Your **name exactly as it appears in your passport**
- Your **affiliation and job title**
- Your **passport number**
- The **country that issued your passport**
- Your **date of birth**
- Your **paper title**, if you are an author
- Whether you also need a **business registration certificate** or other
  supporting document

An invitation letter is a statement that you are expected at the conference. It
does not guarantee a visa will be granted, and ISPASS cannot intervene in visa
decisions.

## If your visa is refused

If you cannot obtain a visa in time, contact the General Chair. Whether ISPASS
{{YEAR}} can accommodate a remote or pre-recorded presentation is for the
Organizing Committee to decide.
