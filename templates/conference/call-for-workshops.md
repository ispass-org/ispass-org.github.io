ISPASS {{YEAR}} will host both **workshops** and **tutorials** at the conference
venue. Their purpose is to provide a forum for exchanging ideas and preliminary
results on topics related to ISPASS, in an interactive setting. We invite
proposals from both academia and industry.

A workshop or tutorial associated with ISPASS {{YEAR}} may be **half a day or a full
day** in length.

## What every proposal must contain

Submit a **1–2 page PDF** containing:

- **Type** — workshop or tutorial
- **Title**
- **Abstract** — 150 to 200 words describing the event, suitable for publication
  on the conference website
- **Contact information** of the workshop or tutorial chair(s)
- **Duration** — half day or full day
- **Expected number of participants**
- **Advertisement** — how you plan to solicit participation, for example
  through social media, a dedicated website, or mailing lists
- **History** — attendance and submission numbers for past editions of the same
  workshop or tutorial, at this or other conferences, if any

## Additional contents for a workshop proposal

- A sample call for papers, including title, scope, format and main topics
- Workshop deadlines
- Invited keynote speakers, if any
- Panel discussion, if any
- Organizers' biographies and affiliations
- A tentative list of programme committee members
- Whether selected papers will be published, and if so where and how

## Additional contents for a tutorial proposal

- Rationale for co-locating with ISPASS
- An outline of the tutorial content, objectives and speakers
- Prerequisite knowledge expected of attendees
- Special requirements, if any
- A biography of the organizers and their relevant experience on the topic

## Where to send it

Email the proposal as a PDF to the ISPASS {{YEAR}} Workshops and Tutorials Chair,
with `[ISPASS{{YEAR}}-WT]` at the start of the subject line. The address will be
published here once the chair is appointed.
