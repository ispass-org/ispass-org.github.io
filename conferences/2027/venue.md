## Getting there

### Airports

New York City is served by three major airports:

- **John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)** — Queens, NY
- **LaGuardia Airport (LGA)** — Queens, NY
- **Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR)** — Newark, NJ

All three connect to Manhattan by public transit, taxi and ride-hailing.
Specific routes and journey times from the conference venue will be published
here once the venue is confirmed.

### Local transit

Manhattan is served by the **MTA subway and bus network**, which runs 24 hours.
A contactless payment card or phone can be tapped directly at subway turnstiles
and on buses (OMNY); no separate ticket is needed.
