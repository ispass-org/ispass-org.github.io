The IEEE International Symposium on Performance Analysis of Systems and Software
(ISPASS) is the leading forum for research on the measurement, characterization,
modelling and simulation of computer systems and software. It brings together
researchers and practitioners who care about how systems actually behave, rather
than only about how they are designed.

ISPASS 2027 is hosted by **New York University** in **New York City, USA**.

## What happens at ISPASS

A typical ISPASS runs for three days:

- **A day of workshops and tutorials**, proposed by the community and selected
  by the Workshops and Tutorials Chair.
- **Two days of main conference**, with single-track keynotes, parallel paper
  sessions, and a poster session for late-breaking work.
- **A banquet**, at which the best paper and distinguished artifact awards are
  presented.

## What ISPASS publishes

ISPASS accepts three kinds of contribution:

- **Research papers** on performance analysis methodology and on the analysis of
  real and experimental systems.
- **Tool and benchmark papers**, judged primarily on their potential to enable
  future research. Authors of accepted tool and benchmark papers are expected to
  open-source their work before the conference.
- **Extended poster abstracts**, for ongoing or late-breaking results.

Papers appear in the IEEE Xplore digital library. ISPASS runs an optional
[artifact evaluation](/2027/artifact-evaluation/) process, and awards IEEE
reproducibility badges to papers whose artifacts pass.

## Sponsorship

ISPASS is sponsored by the IEEE Computer Society, and in past years has been
supported by its Technical Committee on Computer Architecture (TCCA) and its
Technical Committee on Microarchitecture (TCuARCH), alongside industrial
sponsors. Sponsorship for ISPASS 2027 is being arranged; see
[Sponsors](/2027/sponsors/).
