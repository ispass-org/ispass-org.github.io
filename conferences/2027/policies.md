ISPASS 2027 is an IEEE-sponsored conference, and the following policies apply to
every attendee, speaker, organizer and sponsor representative.

## IEEE Event Conduct and Safety Statement

ISPASS 2027 fully supports the [IEEE Event Conduct and Safety
Statement](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/event-conduct-and-safety-statement.html).

IEEE events are intended to provide a safe, productive and welcoming environment
for all participants. Harassment and other inappropriate behaviour are not
tolerated.

## IEEE Computer Society Open Conference Statement

ISPASS 2027 endorses the [IEEE Computer Society Open Conference
Statement](https://www.computer.org/about/policies/open-conference-statement),
which affirms that IEEE Computer Society conferences are open to all who wish to
attend, regardless of nationality, and that the Society works to hold events in
places where attendees can obtain visas.

## Reporting a problem

If something happens at ISPASS 2027 that these policies cover, speak to any
member of the [Organizing Committee](/2027/committee/). Reports can also be made
directly to IEEE through the contacts listed in the Event Conduct and Safety
Statement.

## Data and privacy

This website sets no cookies, includes no analytics or advertising trackers, and
sends no data to third parties. External systems linked from this site —
registration, paper submission and the IEEE digital library — operate under
their own privacy policies.
