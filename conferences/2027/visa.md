:::warning
**Start early.** US visa interview wait times vary enormously by country and can
run to several months. Begin the process as soon as you intend to attend, and do
not wait for a paper acceptance: accepted papers must be presented in person.
:::

ISPASS 2027 will be held in New York City, USA. Whether you need a visa depends
on your nationality and the purpose and length of your stay.

## Visa Waiver Program and ESTA

Citizens of countries in the **Visa Waiver Program (VWP)** may travel to the
United States for business or tourism for up to 90 days without a visa, but must
obtain an approved **ESTA** (Electronic System for Travel Authorization) before
boarding.

- [Check whether your country participates in the VWP](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visa-waiver-program.html)
- [Apply for ESTA](https://esta.cbp.dhs.gov/)

:::warning
Apply for ESTA through the **official US government site** linked above. Many
third-party sites charge a substantial fee for the same application.
:::

An approved ESTA is generally valid for two years and allows multiple entries.

## If you need a visa

If you are not eligible to travel under the VWP, you will normally need a
**B-1 (business) visitor visa**, which covers attending a scientific or
professional conference.

1. Complete the DS-160 online application.
2. Pay the application fee.
3. Schedule an interview at the US embassy or consulate in your country.

Check current wait times for your location before booking travel:
[US visa appointment wait times](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/wait-times.html).

## Invitation letters

ISPASS 2027 can provide a letter of invitation to support a visa application.

:::note
The contact address for invitation letters will be published here once the
ISPASS 2027 General Chair confirms who handles them. In past years this has been
the General Chair.
:::

When requesting an invitation letter, include:

- Your **name exactly as it appears in your passport**
- Your **affiliation and job title**
- Your **passport number**
- The **country that issued your passport**
- Your **date of birth**
- Your **paper title**, if you are an author
- Whether you also need a **business registration certificate** or other
  supporting document

An invitation letter is a statement that you are expected at the conference. It
does not guarantee a visa will be granted, and ISPASS cannot intervene in visa
decisions.

## If your visa is refused

Accepted papers must be presented in person. Authors who need a visa to
present must submit their application no later than **two weeks after the
acceptance notification**, and keep proof of the application date.

If your visa is refused, or will not be issued before the conference, contact
the Program Chair **as soon as possible** with evidence of when you requested an
invitation letter and applied for the visa. In exceptional circumstances like
these the General and Program Chairs may allow a remote presentation, decided
case by case. If a co-author can travel instead, they may present the paper.
See the [in-person presentation policy](/2027/cfp/#in-person-presentation).
