## Requirements

Please make sure that your submission satisfies all of the following.

- **An abstract is required.** You will not be able to submit a full paper
  without first registering an abstract by the abstract deadline.
- The paper must be **original material** that has not been previously published
  in another conference or journal, and is not currently under review elsewhere.
  Material presented previously at a workshop without copyrighted proceedings
  may be submitted.
- The content of the submission is limited to **nine (9) single-spaced,
  double-column pages** using 10-point type on 8.5×11 inch pages (IEEE
  conference style), with an **unlimited number of additional pages for
  references**. All content other than references — figures, appendices and so
  on — must fit within the first nine pages. Papers that exceed the length limit
  or deviate from the formatting requirements may be rejected without review.
- References must **list all authors** for any reference with fewer than ten
  co-authors.
- Papers are reviewed **double-blind**. Author names and other hints of identity
  must be removed from the submitted paper.
- Do **not** omit references in order to preserve anonymity — that leaves
  reviewers unable to grasp the context. Cite your own work in the third person,
  as you would cite anyone else's.
- Please **number the pages** of the submission.
- The paper must be submitted as a **PDF**. No other format can be accepted, and
  the document must print exactly as received.

For tool and benchmark papers, tick the tool/benchmark box in the paper topics.
Do not include a URL to the tool or benchmark if it would reveal your identity;
state instead that you intend to open-source it on acceptance.

Use the IEEE conference style templates for Word and LaTeX provided by IEEE
eXpress Conference Publishing:

[IEEE conference templates](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html)

## Policy on arXiv posting

You may submit a paper already posted to arXiv, or post your paper to arXiv
after submitting it. The submission system allows you to record the arXiv link.

- If you post to arXiv **after** submitting, notify the Program Chair.
- We recommend using **different titles** for the different versions of the
  paper.
- We expect authors to **refrain from advertising the work on social media**
  during the evaluation period.

## Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists with:

- Your PhD advisor and your PhD students, in perpetuity.
- Family relations by blood or marriage, in perpetuity.
- People with whom you have collaborated in the past five years. Collaborators
  include co-authors on accepted, rejected or pending research papers, co-PIs on
  accepted, rejected or pending grants, those who fund your research, and
  researchers whom you fund.
  - *Service* collaborations, such as co-writing a report or serving together on
    a program committee, are not by themselves a conflict of interest.
- Anyone else with whom you believe a conflict exists. If in doubt, ask the
  Program Chair.
