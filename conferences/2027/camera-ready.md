## Before you start

:::warning
**At least one author must pre-register** for the symposium, and one of the
authors must **present the paper in person** in New York City. Remote and
pre-recorded presentations are not accepted. Papers without a pre-registered
author by the camera-ready deadline, and papers that are not presented in
person at the conference, are not included in the proceedings or in IEEE
Xplore.
:::

All authors of accepted papers must complete the **copyright transfer** before
submitting final materials to IEEE. The submission site redirects you to the
copyright form during the process.

## Formatting

Use the same format as the initial submission — see the [IEEE conference
templates](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html) — and
check the following.

- **Paper size: US Letter, 8.5 × 11.0 inches.** A4 may **not** be used. Verify
  the final size in your PDF reader under File → Properties → Description.
- Use the template's **conference mode**.
- Add author information using the template's predefined author block.
- **Margins:** top and bottom 1.00″, sides 0.75″.
- **Font size:** no smaller than 10pt for body text.
- **Line spacing:** single-spaced.
- The paper must be formatted for **black-and-white printers**. Check that
  figures remain readable when printed in greyscale.
- **Do not number the pages**, and do not add headers or footers of any kind.

## Page limits

- Regular paper **without** artifact evaluation: **10 pages**.
- Regular paper **with** artifact evaluation: **12 pages** — the 10-page limit
  plus a two-page artifact appendix.
- References do not count towards the limit; an unlimited number of additional
  pages is allowed for references.
- **No extra pages may be purchased.**

If your paper includes an artifact appendix, order the content as:
**paper contents → references → artifact evaluation appendix**, with no gaps
inserted between the parts.

## Validate the PDF with IEEE PDF eXpress

IEEE requires the final PDF to be Xplore-compatible. Validate it with
[PDF eXpress](https://ieee-pdf-express.org/) before submitting:

1. Choose "First time logging in to PDF eXpress".
2. Enter the ISPASS 2027 **Conference ID** (announced with the camera-ready
   instructions).
3. Enter your email address and choose a password.
4. Upload either your source files for conversion, or a PDF for checking.
5. If the system reports a problem you will receive an email describing it;
   otherwise it emails you an Xplore-compatible PDF.
