The IEEE International Symposium on Performance Analysis of Systems and Software
(ISPASS) has been held annually since 2000. It is the leading forum for research
on the **measurement, characterization, modelling and simulation** of computer
systems and software — work concerned with how systems actually behave, rather
than only with how they are designed.

## Scope

ISPASS covers performance and efficiency analysis across the stack:

- **Methodology** — analytical modelling, statistical methods, simulation
  techniques, tracing and profiling tools, hardware performance counters, and
  power, thermal, variability and reliability models.
- **Hardware** — multicore and many-core processors, accelerators and GPUs,
  memory and storage systems, embedded and mobile systems, datacenters, HPC,
  networks, quantum computing and emerging technologies.
- **Workloads and software** — managed languages, virtualization, datacenter and
  internet-sector workloads, multimedia and games, and machine learning training
  and inference.
- **Foundations** — metrics, bottleneck identification, and visualization.

ISPASS explicitly welcomes **confirmations or refutations of important prior
results**, and **tool and benchmark papers** judged on their potential to enable
future research.

## What ISPASS publishes

Proceedings appear in the **IEEE Xplore digital library**. ISPASS runs an
optional **artifact evaluation** process and awards IEEE reproducibility badges
— *Available*, *Reviewed* and *Reproducible* — to papers whose artifacts are
evaluated successfully.

Alongside full papers, ISPASS accepts **extended poster abstracts** for ongoing
and late-breaking work.

## How ISPASS is organized

Each edition has its own **Organizing Committee**, led by a General Chair and a
Program Chair, who are appointed by the ISPASS **Steering Committee**. Each
edition runs its own review process, registration and website. This site hosts
the current edition and links to the archives of previous ones.

ISPASS is sponsored by the **IEEE Computer Society**, and has been supported by
its Technical Committee on Computer Architecture (TCCA) and its Technical
Committee on Microarchitecture (TCuARCH).

## Getting involved

- **Submit** — each year's Call for Papers opens in the autumn for a conference
  the following spring.
- **Review** — contact the Program Chair of the current edition to volunteer for
  the programme committee.
- **Sponsor** — contact the current edition's Sponsorship Chair.
- **Host** — the Steering Committee selects the host institution for each
  edition several years in advance.
