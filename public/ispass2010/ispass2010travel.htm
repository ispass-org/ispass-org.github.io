Travel Information

 

Area Airports

  • The Westchester county airport (HPN): closest airport, approximately 8 miles (15 mins) from the conference hotel. The hotel offers a complimentary airport shuttle serving HPN.
  • La Guardia airport (LGA): 25 miles (40 mins, more with traffic)
  • John F. Kennedy airport (JFK): 35 miles (45 mins, more with traffic)

Rental cars, one of the several airport shuttles (costing between $50 - $80), or the train from Grand Central (if you are traveling light and have time to spare) are the options to get to the hotel from LGA and JFK. White Plains is a relatively walking-friendly city once you get there.

Train to Manhattan


Grand Central Terminal in Manhattan is 35 minutes away via the Metro North commuter railroad (Harlem line). The Metro North White Plains station is approximately 2 miles away from the hotel (Note that there is also a North White Plains station, which is farther away from the hotel)
