STUDENT TRAVEL GRANT

 

 

The ISPASS 2010 organizers would like to encourage graduate student participation at the conference by partially funding the travel costs.

 

  • Information for Travel Grants:

    • Students from underrepresented groups and/or small institutions are encouraged to apply

    • We also encourage non-authors to apply

    • Since the funding source is from NSF, it can only be used on students at US universities (but no requirement about US citizenship or permanent residents)

    • Deadline: March 1, 2010

    • We will make decisions by March 9, 2010

     

  • How to apply for student travel grant:

    • Please send below information to the Student Travel Chair, Suzanne Rivoire ( suzanne.rivoire @ sonoma.edu ). Also, please CC your advisor.

    • Use the subject line "ISPASS 2010 Student Travel Grant Application"

    • Information to be sent:

               -Name:

               -Institution:

               -Address:

               -Phone Number:

               -Email address:

               -Advisor:
