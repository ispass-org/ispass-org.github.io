Information for Visa Letter Request

 

If you require an invitation letter for purposes of a visa application, please send an email with your name, email address, mailing address, IEEE member number (if any), the ID of the paper you are presenting (if any) and a copy of your registration confirmation to  "registration@ispass.org".


Please note that we can only issue visa letters to speakers/presenters or attendees who have paid their registration fee in full and are not from one of the countries embargoed by the U.S. Treasury Department’s Office of Foreign Assets Control. See IEEE visa request process regulations and the U.S. Department of State web site for more information.
