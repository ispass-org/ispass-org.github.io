STUDENT TRAVEL GRANT

 

 

The ISPASS 2012 organizers would like to encourage graduate student participation at the conference by partially funding students' travel costs. We particularly encourage applications from members of underrepresented groups and/or students at small institutions. Please note that some of our funding sources are restricted to students at US universities and give preference to US citizens and permanent residents.

To apply, please send the information below to the Student Travel Grant Chair, Suzanne Rivoire (suzanne.rivoire@sonoma.edu). Use the subject line "ISPASS 2012 Student Travel Grant Application."

  • In the body of the email, please provide the following information:
    • Name:
    • Institution:
    • Mailing address:
    • Telephone number:
    • Email address:
    • Advisor's name and email address:
    • Reason for attending ISPASS (if presenting a paper or poster, please state the title):
    • Citizenship information (US citizen/US permanent resident/other)
    • [optional] Underrepresented group status (women, persons with disabilities, African Americans, Hispanics/Latinos, American Indians/Alaska natives, or Native Hawaiians/Other Pacific Islanders):
  • Please attach a resume (in pdf format).

You should receive confirmation of your application within two business days. We will contact your advisor to confirm your eligibility for this grant.

Applications are due Wednesday, February 29, 2012.
