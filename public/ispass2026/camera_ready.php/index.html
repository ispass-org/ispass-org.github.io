Detailed instructions

  • Please use the same format as the initial submission for the camera-ready version (refer to the IEEE templates). Please ensure the following:
    • Paper size: US Letter - 8.5 x 11.0 inches. The A4 size may NOT be used. Be sure to check the final size of the PDF from within Adobe Reader (for example) by looking at the info in: "File>Properties>Description".
    • Be sure to use the template's conference mode.
    • Please add author information following the pre-defined author description format in the template.
    • Margins: top/bottom - 1.00", sides - 3/4" (0.75")
    • Font sizes: no smaller than 10pt for body text.
    • Line spacing for body text: single-spaced.
    • The submission must be formatted for black-and-white printers. Please make sure your figures are readable when printed in black-and-white.
    • Do NOT number the pages. The submission should not contain headers or footers of any type.
  • The total page limits for the regular paper WITHOUT ARTIFACT EVALUATION is 10 pages.
    • The page limit does not include pages for references. An unlimited number of additional pages is allowed for references.
    • For authors participating in the artifact evaluation process, an additional two-page appendix for artifact evaluation is allowed. (12 pages in total).
    • No extra pages are available.
  • All authors of accepted papers must complete the copyright transfer before submitting the final materials to IEEE.
    • During the paper submission process, the submission site will redirect you to complete the copyright transfer form.
