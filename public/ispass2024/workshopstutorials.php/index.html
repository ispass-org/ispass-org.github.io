•Introduction to and Exploration of the Structural Simulation Toolkit (SST)
 Organizers: Patrick Lavin (Sandia National Laboratories), Joseph Kenny (Sandia National Laboratories), and Clay Hughes (Sandia National Laboratories)
 Time: 08:00-12:00 (Sunday, May 5th)
 Website: https://sst-simulator.org/SSTPages/SSTTutorial_ISPASS2024/
 Summary: This tutorial will begin with an introduction to the core concepts in SST and a tour of the many architectural simulation components available in SST-Elements. We will then cover two in-depth examples: single-node, multi-core simulation using memHierarchy, and network simulation using Ember, Firefly, and Merlin.
