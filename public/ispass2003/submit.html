Information for Presenters

The primary means of presentation will be via computer projection.  ISPASS will provide an LCD projector, so you can use your own computer using your preferred presentation program for preparation of slides.  (If you require other AV equipment, please contact the organizers to check availability.)

Instructions for Preparation and Mailing of Final, Camera-Ready Version

The final, camera-ready versions of all papers are due NO LATER THAN January 15, 2003. Each paper must be accompanied by: 

A signed IEEE Copyright form (http://computer.org/copyright.htm) to be faxed to the Publications Chair, Prof. David August at (609) 258 1771. Proof of at least one full-rate (non-student) conference pre-registration unless the paper is a student-only submission. Please visit http://ispass.org for registration information. Extra page charges, if applicable, are due together with registration @ U.S. $100 per extra page. 

The final version should be prepared in accordance with standard IEEE Computer Society conference 2-column format (see: http://computer.org/cspress/instruct.htm) and must be e-mailed in PDF format to the Publications Chair, Prof. David August.  You will receive an email acknowledging within 2 days of receipt of your submission.  If  you do not receive such mail, please contact the Publications Chair to ensure your paper has been received.

Page Limits and Extra Page Charges

Accepted papers are allowed up to 10 pages in the conference proceedings. If your paper exceeds the prescribed page limits, extra page charges (U.S. dollars one hundred, $100 per extra page) will apply.   Extra pages are limited to two additional pages per paper.

Page charges can be paid together with the registration and must be received by January 15, 2003 to ensure inclusion of your manuscript in the proceedings.  Authors can fax their registration form to the Finance Chair, Dr. Nadeem Malik, at (512) 823 9478, or via US mail to:
