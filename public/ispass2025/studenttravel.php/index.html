ISPASS 2025 Student Travel Grants

With generous support from IEEE TCCA and IEEE TCuARCH, ISPASS 2025 offers travel grants for students to defray a portion of their travel costs to both U.S. and international student participants. The size and number of these grants will vary depending on funding availability, the number of student applicants, and their respective priority. We particularly encourage students from groups that are underrepresented in the field of computer science to apply.

Important dates and information about student travel grants:

  • Student Travel Grant Application Deadline: March 31, 2025 (AoE).
  • Notification of Grant: April 8, 2025.
  • Grantees must keep original receipts for reimbursement.
  • Expenses will be reimbursed after the conference.
  • Awardees from U.S. schools must fly with U.S.-based carriers for airfare reimbursement.
  • Awardees will be required to email the travel grant chair a short essay describing their ISPASS experience. The essay should describe what they have learned.

How to apply for student travel grant:

  • Student applicants should use this online form to apply for an ISPASS student travel grant.
  • In the form, the students need to upload a letter from their advisor. The letter should state that you are a full-time student pursuing an B.S./M.S./Ph.D. in the areas covered by ISPASS. The advisor is also welcome to comment on the potential broader impact of making an award to you. The form includes a suggested template for the letter.
  • Please contact the travel grant chair, Bahar Asgari bahar@umd.edu, if you have any questions.
