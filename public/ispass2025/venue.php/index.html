Conference location

The ISPASS 2025 main conference on Monday May 12 and Tuesday May 13 will be held at the former Augustinian Monastery in Ghent, Belgium.
The tutorials on Sunday May 11 will be held in the Ghent Marriott Hotel.

There is no reserved room block at a hotel, but there are many hotels for different budgets within walking distance from both venues (a very nice walk through medieval Ghent). The Augustinian Monastery has no hotel rooms. Here is a non-restrictive list of nearby hotels. Don't wait too long to reserve a room, Ghent attracts a lot of tourists in Spring.

  • Ghent Marriott Hotel
  • Hotel Gravensteen
  • Comic Art Hotel
  • Hotel Harmony
  • 1898 The Post
  • Novotel Ghent Centre
  • Hotel De Flandre
  • Low budget: Hostel De Draecke

How to get there

By plane: Fly to Brussels Airport (avoid the "Brussels South" airport, that is actually more than 70 km from Brussels with no good ground transport connections). At the basement of the arrival level, you can take a train to Ghent. Tickets are available at the vending machines, buy a ticket to Ghent (Gent-Sint-Pieters, the main train station in Ghent). There are regular trains that drive directly to Ghent (through Brussels, take the train with terminus Knokke or Blankenberge). If there is no direct train, take the train to Brussels, there are more regular trains from the Brussels train stations (all three) to Ghent. You can switch trains with the same ticket. You can also take a taxi or shared ride, but that will cost you a lot more (the train is about EUR 20, taxi is around EUR 150-200).

By train: Brussels is connected by train from the neighboring European capitals (Paris, London, Amsterdam). As an alternative to flying to Brussels, you can fly to one of those cities and take the train (it is recommended to reserve for international train trips, you can book a ticket directly to Ghent, see https://www.b-europe.com/EN). In Brussels, take the train to Ghent (station Gent-Sint-Pieters). Belgian train website: https://www.belgiantrain.be/en.

By car: You can rent a car at the airport, but the city center of Ghent is car-free, and parking is expensive. Contact your hotel to find the closest parking opportunity. It usually makes no sense to go from the hotel to the venue by car, the closest parking space might be as far as your hotel.

In Ghent: Ghent is a pretty compact city, you can walk to most locations. Getting to your hotel from the train might be a long walk, especially with luggage. Their are taxis in front of the train station, or you can take public transport (https://www.delijn.be/en/). You can take tram 1 or 3 to the city centre. Paying for public transport is straightforward: just hold your contactless credit card or smartphone in front of the terminal in the tram or bus (https://www.delijn.be/en/content/vervoerbewijzen/tickets/ticket/). If there is a ticket check, show them the same means you used to pay, and they can check the validity of your ticket. If you plan multiple trips during your stay, it might be more beneficial to buy a 10-journey ticket (online https://www.delijn.be/en/tickets/ or at the vending machines).
