Visa Support Letter Requests

Visa support letters can be provided to papers' authors, presenters and speakers. For visa support letters, refer all requests to the General Chair Michela Becchi. Use "ISPASS 2023 VISA" as email subject. All requests are handled in the order they are received. The information below should be included with the request:

  • Your name as it appears on your passport
  • Your current postal mailing address
  • If you have any papers accepted for the conference, please provide the title and indicate whether you are the “sole author” or a “co-author”
  • If you are a speaker, please provide the title of their presentation
  • Your registration confirmation number

ISPASS does not provide letters for transport of vendor or presenter equipment.
