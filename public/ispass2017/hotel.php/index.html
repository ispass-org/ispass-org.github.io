Getting There

The closest airport is the Charles M. Schulz-Sonoma County Airport (STS), which is 8 miles from the hotel. The hotel website lists taxi services and car rental companies that serve this airport.

However, STS is small, and flights can be expensive. Two larger airports that are 60-90 minutes away by car are San Francisco International Airport (SFO) and Oakland International Airport (OAK). The easiest way to get from these airports to the hotel is to either rent a car or use Sonoma County Airport Express. As the Airport Express approaches Sonoma County, the driver will ask if any passengers need a taxi at their stops and will generally make sure the taxi arrives. The closest Airport Express stop to the hotel is the Santa Rosa Park and Ride (less than 2 miles).
