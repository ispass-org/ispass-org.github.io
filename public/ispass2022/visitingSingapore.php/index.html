Visiting Singapore for ISPASS 2022


ISPASS in Singapore

We will host ISPASS 2022 at the National University of Singapore (NUS) campus in Singapore. We hope that the vast majority of attendees will be able to come to Singapore and attend in person, but we do understand that there can be extreme cases where there can be issues for travel (Visa issues, COVID diagnosis, etc.). Therefore, we aim to accommodate most people in person. If, because of extenuating circumstances, you will not be able to attend, please indicate that information here.


ISPASS Conference Fees

For a paper or an extended abstract to appear in the proceedings, at least one author must register for the ISPASS conference and attend the conference to present the paper or poster. Overall, our goal is to maintain low conference fees to enable most people to visit Singapore. We expect that IEEE member registration will be less than USD $350, with student prices being less.


Travel to Singapore (Updated: May 4th 2022)

Travel to Singapore has recently been simplified. No pre-departure COVID-19 test is required for fully-vaccinated visitors. For more details on the requirements for fully-vaccinated and non-vaccinated visitors please check here. More specifically, check requirements for arriving and departing. Note that the normal visa process applies. You can find out if your citizenship requires a visa here, but citizens from a number of countries will not need to apply for a visa.

Travel in Singapore

Bus travel is extensive, affordable and convenient in Singapore (Google Maps has real-time bus and metro data, and you can pay for your ride with your credit card). In addition, travel by taxi or ride-share apps (Grab app or GoJek app) is fast and very reasonably priced. Using the ComfortDelgo Taxi app a recent ride from the airport to NUS was USD $18.00 (33 km; ca. 30 minutes), and a shorter 6.5km ride was USD $7.80. Taxis and ride-shares can be paid for by credit card through the app; taxis also accept cash payment.
