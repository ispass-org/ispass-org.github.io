Visa Requirements

The ISPASS-2022 conference will be held in Singapore. A visa may be required for entering the country. For the most up-to-date information on whether you need a visa to attend ISPASS-2022, check the Singapore Immigration and Checkpoints Authority.

Once you register for the conference, ISPASS-2022 is able to provide visa support letters to attendees as well as authors with accepted papers or members of the conference committee.



Visa Support Letter Requests

For visa support letters, refer all requests to the General Chair Trevor E. Carlson. Please allow up to 10 business days to receive a letter. All requests are handled in the order they are received. The information below should be included with the request:

  • Your name as it appears on your passport
  • Your current postal mailing address
  • Your registration confirmation number
  • If you have any papers accepted for the conference, please provide the title and indicate whether you are the “sole author” or a “co-author”
  • Authors may indicate their paper title. If no paper, speakers can provide the title of their presentation.

ISPASS does not provide letters for transport of vendor or presenter equipment. ISPASS suggests shipping the materials insured to the conference facility.
