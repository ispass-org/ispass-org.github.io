ISPASS-2000
IEEE International Symposium on Performance Analysis of Systems and Software
April 24-25, 2000
Austin, Texas, USA
The 2000 IEEE International Symposium on Performance Analysis of Systems and Software is sponsored by the IEEE Computer Society's Technical Committee on Internet. This annual international conference provides a forum for sharing advanced and well-established academic and industrial R&D work focused on performance analysis in the design of systems and software. A high standard has been set by the conference to select only a small number of papers that exhibit a quality level approaching that of research journals, but without the long turnaround time, to allow quick dissemination of results.

ISPASS-2000 will be held in Austin, Texas, from April 24 through April 25, 2000 at the Radisson Hotel and Suites in downtown Austin overlooking the town lake.
