ISPASS 2021 Student Participation Grants

ISPASS 2021 is offering participation grants, in lieu with travel grants, that will be used to cover registration fees. The grants have been made possible through generous support from IEEE TCCA.

Please follow these steps to apply:

  • Fill the following google form (requires uploading CV/resume): https://forms.gle/DJmKzvxwc33fNmbx7
  • Have your advisor confirm your eligibility (full-time student working on ISPASS-related research topics) for ISPASS participation grant by sending an email to Amro Awad with the title [Confirming the Eligibility of {Your Name} for the ISPASS Participation Grant]

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact the participation grant chair: Amro Awad.


Important Dates

  • Application submission and advisor's confirmation: March 7th, 2021, 11:59:59 PM, Eastern Time
