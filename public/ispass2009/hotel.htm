HOTEL/TRAVEL INFORMATION

 

ISPASS 2009 will be held at Radisson Hotel Boston

  • Address: 200 Stuart Street, Boston, MA 02116

  • Contact:  (Phone) 617-482-1800 or 1-800-333-3333

  • Rates and Reservation (extended cutoff date: April  10th, Friday  4th Saturday)

    • Rate: $169/night for single/double, $199 for triple, and $219 for quad.

  • Map information

  • Room Reservation Procedure:
    • It is the Hotel’s understanding that individuals will make their own reservations by calling directly to our Reservation Department at 617-482-1800 or by calling Radisson Worldwide at 1-800-333-3333.  In order to receive your special rate, individuals must indicate that they are with the IEEE International Symposium on Performance Analysis of Systems and Software group (ISPASS 2009).  Individuals responsible for making their own reservations will be asked to guarantee for late arrival with a major credit card.  Should the individual “not show” or cancel by 4:00 PM one day prior to arrival, the individual’s credit card will be charged, or any deposit will be applied for the first night’s room revenue. The room block is being held until Saturday, April 4th.
  • Hotel parking information:
    • Overnight parking for guests staying at the Hotel is available for $31.00 self and $33.00 valet parking
    • Function parking is available for your event at a charge of $14.00 self and $16.00 valet parking, and is available one hour prior to posted start time of the event and one hour after the posted end time of the event. Stickers will be available the day of your event. Please note, this function parking price is NOT available for overnight parking.
