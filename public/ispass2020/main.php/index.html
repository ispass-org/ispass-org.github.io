Dear ISPASS 2020 Community,

Due to the continuing impact of the COVID-19 pandemic, the ISPASS Organizing Committee, ISPASS Steering Committee, and IEEE CS have made the disappointing decision to move ISPASS 2020 to a virtual conference format occurring August 23-26, 2020. We are currently working with IEEE CS to determine how best to turn ISPASS 2020 into a successful virtual conference and will update the community with those plans in the near future.

We were very much looking forward to hosting ISPASS in the beautiful and historic city of Boston and are disappointed that we will not be able to meet in person this year. However, we look forward to enabling a vibrant discussion of the research being presented by so many wonderful researchers in this year's virtual conference setting.

If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Sincerely,

David Kaeli
Kelly Shaw
