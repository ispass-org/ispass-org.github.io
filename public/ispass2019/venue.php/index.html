1 John Nolen Drive, Madison, WI

About the Monona Terrace

The Monona Terrace is a conference center on the short of Lake Monona in downtown Madison. The building was designed by Frank Lloyd Wright. It is directly connected to the Hilton Monona Terrace, where we have a reserved room block for the conference.
https://www.mononaterrace.com/

Getting to Madison

Madison is served directly by MSN airport, which is about a 10 minute taxi/ride-share from the conference venue. Alternatively, one can fly to Milwaukee (MKE - 1.5 hr) or Chicago O'Hare (ORD - 3 hr.) and either drive or take a bus to Madison.
