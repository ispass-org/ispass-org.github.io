ISPASS Travel and Visas

Getting to Madison

Madison is served directly by MSN airport, which is about a 10 minute taxi/ride-share from the conference venue. Alternatively, one can fly to Milwaukee (MKE - 1.5 hr) or Chicago O'Hare (ORD - 3 hr.) and either drive or take a bus to Madison.

Visa Procedures

If you require a Visa to enter the United States, we encourage you to apply as soon as possible. If you require an invitation letter, please register for the conference and then contact the General Chair at twenisch@umich.edu
