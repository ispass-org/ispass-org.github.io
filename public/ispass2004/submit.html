SUBMISSION GUIDELINE

Papers should be prepared to the standard IEEE Computer Society 2-column format. Formatting guidelines are available at 

 

     http://computer.org/cspress/instruct.htm. 

 

You should convert your document to PDF format ( be sure to embed any special fonts that you use ) and email the document to content@omnipress.com.  Please use the subject line: "IEEE ISPASS PAPER attn Toby". Please also CC Craig Chase ( chase@ece.utexas.edu ) on your email submission.

 

If you have any problems formatting your paper, please contact either Ms. Toby Linzmeier at Omnipress ( linzmeier@omnipress.com, by telephone 800-828-0305 or 1-608-246-2600 ), or contact Craig Chase ( chase@ece.utexas.edu, 1-512-471-7457 ). 

 

Please keep in mind that papers are limited to eight pages in the two column format. Papers longer than eight pages will be charged an extra page fee of $100US per page. At least one author must pre-register for the symposium ( with the non-student rate ), and at least one author must attend the symposium to present the paper. Papers for which no author is pre-registered will be pulled from the proceedings.

 

Also, please send IEEE copyright form along with your registration form.
