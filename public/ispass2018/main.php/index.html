The 2018 IEEE International Symposium on Performance Analysis of Systems and Software is sponsored by the IEEE Computer Society's Technical Committee on Internet, Technical Committee on Computer Architecture, and Technical Committee on Microprogramming and Microarchitecture.

ISPASS 2018 will take place on the campus of Queens University, Belfast, Northern Ireland (UK).
