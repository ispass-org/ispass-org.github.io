HOTEL/TRAVEL INFORMATION

 

ISPASS 2008 will be held at Renaissance Austin Hotel. It is located in northwest Austin near the headquarters to several of the country's largest and emerging technology companies. The hotel is also just 20~30 minutes from the State Capitol building, the University of Texas, LBJ Library and Austin Bergstrom Airport.
